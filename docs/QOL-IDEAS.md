# Quality-of-life ideas (from the 2026-10-31 match to 12 Hegemonia)

Ordered by how often the problem showed up while playing. None of this changes the rules.

1. **Payment in the Compra modal.** Show the cost (with the first-player surcharge), check the Reserva (colour rule, ouro) and subtract the chips; today a player can buy without paying and the leftover counters just vanish at the pass.
2. **Comerciar button.** "2 workers → 1 ouro" is two clicks plus three counter edits (take back the two colours, add the ouro).
3. **Resolve the Stack.** After an Estratégia is played, one button to send the Stack's cards to the Descanso (the native flow needs a target arrow and a menu).
4. **Victory announcement for Combate.** Announce "12 Hegemonia, vitória" when the counter is edited, and a *Combate* button that records the result (attacker, defender, points, fuga halving) instead of typing the counter.
5. **Hand-to-Descanso / Renovação over 6 cards.** The Renovação only warns "descarte N cartas"; offer a picker.
6. **Assalto / Combate helpers:** a button that lists the cards on the Campo per player with their force, sums both sides, applies the muralha (+1) and the dano rule (1 → Descanso, ≥ 2 → Desterro), and sends the cards where the manual says (a player cannot move the other's cards today; ask the owner through a chat button).
7. **Token storage without drag-and-drop:** a "Guardar recurso" panel (colour → Melhoria) so resources, força and muralha can be stored and spent from a list; it would also make the Capital's starting token real.
8. **Colonos: capture.** Let the attacker choose perdoar / destruir / capturar from a panel; capture needs a cross-player move, so it would have to be a request the victim accepts.
9. **One-click turn flow:** "Fim da Campanha" that detects the trégua (everybody passed with no action), runs Final de Campanha + Renovação for both players and rolls the Mercado once, so nobody has to press four buttons or remember the extra pass for the primazia.
10. **Visible Campanha / turn state:** the Reserva already shows the Campanha and the first player; add "turno de X" and the trégua counter ("1 de 2 passes").
11. **Estratégia and Melhoria texts at hand:** a hover card on the board is the only way to read an ability; a "card text" line in the Compra modal and the Colonizar panel would save a lot of zooming.
12. **Smaller layout on small windows:** the Reserva wraps to three lines of buttons and the Colonizar/Compra/Topo panels overlap each other when two are open; group the buttons (Turno / Mercado / Conflito) and keep one panel open at a time.
13. **Log noise:** manual moves print the engine's double "sent X to the top of their Y" lines, and a reshuffle prints every card name; a custom "Embaralhar Descanso" without names, and `noLogs` on the Hegemonia counter's duplicate line, would clean the table's log.
14. **Card ability keywords:** the Léxico (§16) is still empty in the manual, so [LANCEAR], [CARGA], [INVESTIDA]… are applied from the card text by the players; a keyword tooltip on the card would remove the guessing.
